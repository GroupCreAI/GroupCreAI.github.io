Astelle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Astelle Android app. The short version: your birth details never leave your phone. There is no account and no server. The app is funded by ads, which are served by Google AdMob; what AdMob collects is described separately below.

Who is responsible, and how to reach us

Developer and data controller: Zero-Shot
Contact: astelle.destek@gmail.com

What the app keeps on your device

To calculate your chart and personalise your readings, the app uses:

  • Your name or nickname (optional)
  • Your birth date, birth time and birth place
  • The same details for any other profiles you add (partner, family)
  • The wishes you type in the coffee cup reading
  • Your preferences (language, active profile, reading settings and similar)

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's own browser storage. The calculations run on your phone too. The developer does not collect, receive or store this information, and there is no server it could be sent to. You don't need to register or create an account.

The app works offline. If you choose to share a share card or a reading, the content is passed to the app you pick in Android's share menu, and that app handles it from there.

The app deliberately excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your details stay on this phone only. If you switch phones, you will need to enter your profiles again.

How to delete your data

  • In the app, open Profile and tap "Delete all data" to erase everything in one step.
  • Or clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Because the developer holds no copy, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Ads: Google AdMob

The app uses Google's AdMob service to show a small banner at the bottom of the screen and rewarded ads that open only when you choose (for example, to open another coffee cup on the same day). AdMob is Google software that runs inside the app and may send Google:

  • Device identifiers: your advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and app set ID
  • Device and network information (such as device model, OS version, language, carrier and connection type)
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • App and ad interactions (such as app launches, taps, video ad views, which ad was shown and whether it was tapped)
  • Diagnostic information (performance of the app and the ads software, such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage)

Google uses this to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have given consent, to show personalised ads. Your birth details, profiles and wishes are not given to AdMob.

Google is responsible for this data. Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Manage your ad preferences in Google's My Ad Center: https://myadcenter.google.com

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message on first launch. That is where you choose whether to allow personalised ads. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised. If you clear the app's data, the message appears again and you can make a different choice.

Resetting or deleting your advertising ID

On Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID (menu names vary slightly between devices). Once deleted, apps can no longer use it for personalised advertising.

Fonts

The app's fonts are bundled inside the app; no internet connection is made to load them.

Legal basis and your rights

For users in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies; for users in the European Economic Area and the UK, the GDPR / UK GDPR applies.

  • The data on your device is processed only on your phone, to provide the service you asked for (your chart and readings).
  • Showing ads and preventing fraud rely on the legitimate interest in keeping the app funded by advertising. Personalised ads rely on your consent where required, and you can withdraw it at any time.

You have the right to be informed about and access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain. In Turkey you can apply to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Board); in the EEA and UK, to the data protection supervisory authority in your country.

Because the developer keeps no personal data about you, most of these requests are fulfilled fastest right on your device: you can edit or delete your details in Profile. Requests about data processed through AdMob are for Google, and the Google pages and My Ad Center linked above are there for exactly that. You're still welcome to write to astelle.destek@gmail.com; we'll gladly point you in the right direction.

International transfers

The developer does not transfer your data anywhere. However, data sent to Google through AdMob may be processed on Google servers outside Turkey (for example in the United States). Google applies its own safeguards to these transfers, described in Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children or anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone in that age group. Since the app keeps data on the device anyway, if you think a child has been using it, simply delete the data from Profile.

Security

Your data stays on your device, inside Android's app sandbox, and is not sent over the internet. We recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock; anyone holding an unlocked device could see what's in the app.

For entertainment

Planet positions are calculated astronomically; the readings are written for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not medical, legal or financial advice. The app never predicts death, illness or disasters.

Changes to this policy

The app is updated regularly. If an update changes how your data is collected or used, we'll update this policy before that update is released and change the effective date above.
